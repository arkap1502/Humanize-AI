How it works

Three steps, no sign-up, no upload required.

  1. Paste and detect

    Your text is scored live for AI signals: marker phrases, uniform sentences, repeated openers.

  2. Humanize

    Pick light, medium, or strong. Stiff phrasing is rewritten, long sentences split, rhythm varied.

  3. Verify the score

    Watch the AI-likelihood percentage drop and inspect exactly which signals were removed.

Humanize AI runs entirely in your browser. Scores are an estimate, not a guarantee.
